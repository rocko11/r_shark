5. The data and its limits

R Shark compiles public records and government data, including NYC Open Data (ACRIS, PLUTO, DOF, DOB, HPD and others), NYC Planning, New York State and New Jersey parcel, assessment and court records, FEMA, EPA and state environmental agencies. It then adds its own calculations, such as estimated taxes, buildable floor area and deal signals.

Those sources can be incomplete, out of date or wrong. Some records lag by weeks, and agencies change their datasets without notice. Our calculations are estimates based on our reading of the rules. The R Shark AI summary is generated by an AI model from the facts in the report and can contain mistakes. Nothing in R Shark is legal, tax, title, zoning, environmental, engineering, appraisal or investment advice. It doesn't replace a title search, a zoning opinion, a Phase I environmental assessment, a survey or advice from your own professionals. Confirm anything you rely on before you sign, lend or build.
