Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page in one click; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Where does the data come from?

Public government records — NYC Open Data (PLUTO, ACRIS, DOB, HPD, DOF), NYC Planning, NY State and New Jersey parcel and assessment records, FEMA, EPA and NYS / NJ environmental agencies — refreshed live or daily.

Is this legal or investment advice?

No. R Shark is a research and screening tool. Confirm zoning, title and environmental findings with professionals before you buy.

Can I switch plans?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade from your account page; Stripe prorates the difference automatically.
