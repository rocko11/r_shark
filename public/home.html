Questions

Where does the data come from?

Official public records, refreshed daily or live. R Shark's own analysis engine combines them, checks them against each other and does the math for you.

How current is it?

Very current. Violations, permits and complaints update daily, and deed records typically appear within a few weeks of recording.

What does the free preview include?

Every section of the report for any address you run, with the details blurred. Subscribe and the same report unlocks immediately.

Can I cancel or switch plans?

Yes, any time from your account page. Cancelling keeps your access to the end of the period you paid for; switching plans is prorated automatically.

Is this legal or investment advice?

No. R Shark is a research and screening tool. Confirm zoning, title and environmental findings with your attorney, expediter or consultant before you buy.
